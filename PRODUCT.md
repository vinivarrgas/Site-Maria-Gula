# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, no build step (user's choice). Intended for GitHub Pages. Menu, prices and hours live in `js/dados.js` so the owner can edit one file.

## Users

- Locals and summer visitors in Itapema (SC) who reach the site from Instagram (@mariagulagula) on their phones, deciding what sweet or snack to get today, for delivery or pickup.
- People planning a party or special date who want a whole cake, torta or brigadeiros made to order.

## Product Purpose

Demo site for Maria Gula Confeitaria, by Maria Baramarchi: a confeitaria with a shop at Av. Nereu Ramos, 2880, at the start of Meia Praia, Itapema. The site shows the daily delivery menu with prices, lets the visitor build an order and send it ready-made to the shop's WhatsApp, and shows where and when to visit. Success: a visitor goes from Instagram to a complete WhatsApp order in under a minute.

## Positioning

A new neighbourhood shop (opened 26/09/2026) that posts a dated delivery menu in its stories every day ("Seu doce favorito, hoje!") and takes orders on WhatsApp. Its products mix Brazilian party sweets (vulcões, brigadeiros, piscininha) with Santa Catarina's German heritage (cuca alemã).

## Operating Context

- Orders arrive on WhatsApp (47) 99752-4457; delivery fee is quoted in the conversation.
- Shop and delivery hours confirmed by the user from the latest story: shop 8h–18h, delivery 9h–18h. Whether Sunday follows the same hours is not confirmed (older posts said Sunday 10h–18h or 9h30–18h).
- The menu changes with daily production; the current one is the 26/10 delivery menu.

- Menu (26/10): Bolos & Vulcões (Vulcão de cenoura com chocolate R$35, Vulcão de chocolate R$35, Vulcão Choconinho R$35, Piscininha de cenoura R$25); Especiais (Bolo Nuvem, "hypado", R$22); Salgados (Esfiha de carne R$7, Esfiha de frango R$7, Empadão de frango com requeijão R$18); Cucas (Cuca alemã R$25, sabores abacaxi com coco e creme belga / creme belga com chocolate branco); Doces (Brownie tradicional R$7, Cookie tradicional R$14, Big Cookie recheado R$22, sabores Bueno / Nutella / Red Velvet com chocolate branco / Black com marshmallow).
- Also sells espresso coffee in the shop; takes custom orders (tortas, brigadeiros, Easter).
- Custom orders (highlights "Pedidos", "Cardápio Tortas", "Cardápio Doces", seen 27/10/2026): at least 2 days' notice; payment by Pix or card; an order is confirmed with 50% paid upfront; pickup or delivery (fee by address). For a quote she asks for name, event date, number of people, cake and sweets flavours, and accepts a reference photo or lets the chef design it.
- Tortas: 21 flavours sold by the kilo. The highlight footer shows R$ 109,90 and R$ 89,90, partly hidden, so the per-flavour price is unconfirmed. Built with 3 layers of sponge and 2 of filling. Sizes: 15 cm (up to 15 slices), 20 cm (up to 22), 25 cm (up to 32); larger and tiered cakes on request.
- Docinhos: sold in batches of 25 per flavour. Clássicos R$ 2,00 each (R$ 180,00 the hundred), Especiais R$ 2,50 each, Finos from R$ 3,50 to R$ 6,00 each. These menus were posted about 49 weeks ago.

## Product Principles

1. Today's menu and the way to order are never more than one tap away.
2. Every price and hour shown is one the shop published; anything unconfirmed is flagged to the owner.
3. The site should feel like standing in front of her shop, not like a generic bakery template.
4. Works first on a phone held in one hand, in bright daylight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast; every control reachable by keyboard with visible focus; respects reduced motion.
